Tests for package Epi version 2.66
Tests for the twoby2() function for the intended use:
- estimation of the probability difference and its 95% confidence interval
- estimation of the relative risk and its 95% Wald confidence interval
- calculation of the exact p-value using Fisher's exact test
- calculation of the asymptotic p-value
- correct results for small event counts, including zero events
- correct direction of the probability difference and relative risk when treatment groups are reversed
- correct treatment-effect orientation for the intended analysis
